## 11. Domain-Driven Design & Modular Monolith (MEDIUM-HIGH)

Opt-in. Use only for modular monoliths with real business rules. Skip for simple CRUD apps.

- **One Repository per Aggregate, Interface in Domain** — Aggregates are always loaded and saved whole, so their rules cannot be bypassed. [`ddd-aggregate-repository.md`](skills/nestjs-best-practices/rules/ddd-aggregate-repository.md)
- **One Module per Bounded Context with Layers** — Keeps business areas independent so they can change, scale, or split out on their own. [`ddd-bounded-context-modules.md`](skills/nestjs-best-practices/rules/ddd-bounded-context-modules.md)
- **Split Writes and Reads with CQRS** — Writes stay safe through aggregates while reads stay fast and shaped for the screen. [`ddd-cqrs.md`](skills/nestjs-best-practices/rules/ddd-cqrs.md)
- **Modules Talk Only Through a Public Contract or Integration Events** — Stops hidden coupling between modules so one module can change or move out without breaking others. [`ddd-module-public-api.md`](skills/nestjs-best-practices/rules/ddd-module-public-api.md)
- **Keep Business Rules in a Rich Domain Model, Separate from ORM Entities** — Rules live in one place and cannot be skipped; domain tests run without a database. [`ddd-rich-domain-model.md`](skills/nestjs-best-practices/rules/ddd-rich-domain-model.md)
- **Publish Integration Events with a Transactional Outbox** — No lost or phantom events when the app crashes between saving data and publishing. [`ddd-transactional-outbox.md`](skills/nestjs-best-practices/rules/ddd-transactional-outbox.md)
